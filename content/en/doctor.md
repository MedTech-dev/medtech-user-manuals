## Before you start

The doctor portal is a web page. It works on a computer, a tablet or a phone.

- **Your sign-in.** You sign in with your phone number and your password.
- **Your assistants.** An assistant uses the same portal with their own phone number and a password you set for them. They see only the tasks you allow (see *Assistants*, below).
- **Two parts, one menu.** The menu on the left holds your practice — appointments, walk-in patients, patients, clinics, prescriptions — and the Patient Support Programme pages: **Coupon balance**, **Give coupon** and **Cases**.

> [!note] Your work is saved only when you press the button that saves it. If you leave a form half way, nothing is kept. The enrolment, prescription and profile forms warn you before you leave.

## Sign in

::shot sign-in | The sign-in screen.
1. **Phone number** — your mobile number.
2. **Password** — your password.
3. Click the eye to show the password while you type it.
4. **Sign in** opens the portal.
5. Switch the screen between English and العربية at any time.

## Find your way around

The portal opens on **Coupon balance**. The menu is always on the left.

::shot home | The first screen after you sign in.
1. **Coupon balance** — your places on each support programme.
2. **Appointments** — your bookings. Most days start here.
3. **Give coupon** — enrol a patient on a support programme.
4. **Notifications** — your alerts. The red number counts the unread ones.
5. Your name, and your role (*Doctor* or *Assistant*).
6. Switch between a light and a dark screen.
7. The settings menu: **Language** (English or العربية) and **Sign out**. Always sign out on a shared computer.

| Menu | What it is for |

## Appointments

### See your appointments

::shot appointments-list | Appointments, as a list.
1. Choose how to see them: **List**, **Cards** or **Calendar**.
2. Show one stage only: **All**, **Needs approval**, **Confirmed**, **Checked in** or **Completed**.
3. **More filters** — search by patient, or filter by date, clinic, visit type, how it was booked, or payment.
4. Today's walk-in session, with the number of patients present. Click it to open **Walk-in clinic**.
5. The stage of each appointment. *Needs approval* means the patient is waiting for your answer.
6. *PSP* marks a booking linked to a support programme, with the programme's name.

Click any appointment to open it.

::shot appointments-calendar | The same appointments on the calendar.
1. Move to the previous or next day, or jump back with **Today**.
2. **Day** or **Week**.
3. The legend: the colour of an appointment shows its stage. Crossed-out entries were moved, cancelled, rejected or missed.
4. A walk-in session is the dashed block, with the number of patients in it.
5. The red line is the time now.

### Answer a booking request

::shot appointment-request | A booking request.
1. *Needs approval* — the patient is waiting for you.
2. **View patient profile** opens the patient's record.
3. **Call patient** dials the patient's phone.
4. **Approve** confirms the appointment.
5. **Reject** declines it. You must give a reason.
6. **Patient intake** — the answers the patient gave when booking.

::shot appointment-reject | Rejecting a request.
1. **Reject this appointment?** The patient is told and the time is freed.
2. **Reason for rejection** — write something the patient can act on.
3. **Go back** closes the box without changing anything.
4. **Reject** sends the rejection.

### Move the appointment along

The buttons in **Actions** change with the stage of the appointment. You only ever see the buttons that apply now.

| Stage | Buttons you see |
|---|---|
| **Needs approval** | **Approve**, **Reject** |
| **Confirmed** | **Check in** when the patient arrives; **Cancel appointment**; **Mark no-show** |
| **Checked in** | **Start consultation**; **Mark no-show** if the patient left before being seen |
| **In consultation** | **Complete visit** |

> [!warn] **Mark no-show** and **Cancel appointment** ask you to confirm first. A no-show cannot be undone, and the patient's deposit is not refunded.

::shot appointment-consultation | A patient in the consultation room.
1. This booking is linked to a support programme.
2. The countdown of the visit: time left of the appointment length.
3. **Write prescription** (or **Edit prescription** once one exists) — see *Prescriptions*, below.
4. **Complete visit** ends the visit.
5. **Enroll in a support program** — appears once the patient has checked in. It opens the enrolment for this patient.

## Walk-in clinic

A walk-in session is one block of hours for patients without an appointment. You add each patient by phone number as they arrive.

::shot walkin-session | Today's walk-in session.
1. The clinic and the session. *Open* means the session is running; under it you see how many are present and how many were seen.
2. **Phone number** — the patient's mobile.
3. **Name** — if the number already has an account, the patient's own name is used.
4. **Add to today's list** puts the patient in the queue.
5. **Start** calls a waiting patient into the room.
6. **Done** ends the visit of the patient in the room.
7. **Close session** ends the walk-in block.

- Each patient shows *Waiting*, *In the room* or *Seen*, and for how long.
- The bin next to a waiting patient removes them from the list.
- *No account* means the number has no MedTech account yet. The visit joins their record if they sign up later.
- While a patient is in the room, **Prescription** opens their prescription.
- **Schedule a session** sets up a walk-in block for another day. It shows on your calendar.
- If no session is open today, the page offers **Open today's session**: choose the times and press **Open session**.

> [!note] If you close the session while patients are still waiting or in the room, they are left as they are. A visit that did not happen is not recorded.

## Patients

::shot patients-list | Your patients.
1. **Search patients** by name or phone number.
2. Click a patient to open their record.
3. *History shared* — the patient lets you see their medical history.
4. *History not shared* — you see only their basic details and their appointments with you. The patient can give access from their app.

::shot patient-record | A patient's record.
1. Name, age, sex, blood group, height, weight and city.
2. **Allergies** (in red) and **Chronic conditions**.
3. **Call patient**.
4. **Records** — diagnoses, prescriptions, lab results, scans, documents and readings.
5. Each prescription shows the doctor, the date, the instructions and the medicines.

Further down, **Appointments with you** lists every visit; click one to open it.

## Prescriptions

Only a doctor can write a prescription. Open it from the appointment (**Write prescription**) once the patient has checked in, or from **Prescription** in the walk-in list.

::shot rx-page | An empty prescription.
1. The prescription is for this patient and this visit.
2. The patient's allergies and chronic conditions, as a reminder.
3. **Diagnosis (optional)**.
4. **Add medication**. The other sections work the same way: **IV fluids & injections**, **Lab tests** and **Radiology**.
5. **Prescription templates** — reuse a whole prescription you saved before. Each section also has its own **Templates** and **Save as template**.
6. The live preview of the printed prescription, on your letterhead.

::shot rx-medication | Adding a medicine.
1. Type a few letters of the medicine and pick it from the list.
2. **Frequency** — tap one choice.
3. **Duration** — tap one choice.
4. Notes — tap **After meals**, **Before meals** or **At bedtime**, or type your own.
5. The preview updates as you go.

Lab tests and scans offer *Suggested — tap to add* chips. At the bottom, add **General instructions for the patient** and a **Follow-up date** (tap *1 week*, *1 month*…). Then press **Save prescription**.

::shot rx-saved | The saved prescription.
1. The version number. Every save after the first makes a new version.
2. **Open PDF** — the printable prescription. Print or download it from there.
3. **Back to appointment**.

When you save, the patient is told. *Preparing PDF…* shows for a few seconds before **Open PDF** appears. If the PDF cannot be made, the screen says so and offers **Print** and **Try again**.

> [!tip] A prescription needs at least one item or some general instructions. Instructions alone are fine.

## My clinics

::shot clinics-list | Your clinics.
1. **Add clinic** — a new clinic is reviewed by MedTech before patients can book it.
2. Each clinic has a card. Click it, or **Manage**, to open it.
3. *Approved* — patients can book this clinic.
4. *Pending review* — MedTech has not approved it yet.
5. **Manage** opens the clinic's details and hours.

::shot clinic-manage | One clinic.
1. **Edit** changes the name, phone, address and how patients can book here.
2. **Add hours** adds opening times.
3. The pencil changes the hours of one day; the bin next to it removes them.
4. *Closed* — no hours on that day.

::shot clinic-hours | Adding opening hours.
1. **Days** — tap each day these hours apply to.
2. **Select all** picks the whole week.
3. **Opens** — the start time.
4. **Closes** — the end time.
5. **Add hours** saves them. Hours that overlap another shift on the same day are refused, with a message.

::shot clinic-closures | Holidays, closures and fees.
1. **Holidays and closures** — days the clinic is closed.
2. **Add a closure** — pick the date and, if you like, a reason.
3. **Consultation fees** — shown to patients when they book.
4. **Add fee** — the visit type, the amount and how it is paid.

> [!warn] Adding a closure does not cancel the bookings already on that day. Cancel those yourself.

At the bottom, **Remove clinic** stops new bookings. It is refused while the clinic still has upcoming appointments. A licence change goes through a request on your profile.

::shot clinic-new | Adding a clinic.
1. **Clinic name**, **Phone** and **Address**.
2. **Governorate**, then **City**.
3. **Clinic license** — upload the licence document. It is required. The **Clinic logo** is optional.
4. **Add hours** — at least one opening time is required.
5. **Map location** (optional) — tap **Use my current location** at the clinic, or paste a Google Maps link.
6. **Submit for approval** sends the clinic to MedTech.

## Intake questions

Patients answer these questions when they book. Their answers appear on the appointment under **Patient intake**.

::shot questionnaire | Your intake questions.
1. **Questions** — the list, in the order patients see it.
2. *Automatic* — MedTech fills these in from the patient's profile (age, gender, chronic diseases). Patients are not asked them, and you cannot change them.
3. The arrows move a question up or down. The pencil edits it; the bin removes it.
4. **Add choice** adds an answer to a choice question.

::shot question-add | A new question.
1. **Question type** — *Free text*, *Number*, *Yes / no*, *Date*, *Single choice*, *Multiple choice*, *Dropdown* or *File upload*.
2. **Question** — in English.
3. **Question in Arabic** — optional; patients see the English text until you add it.
4. **Patient must answer** makes the question required.
5. **Save**. For a choice question, add the choices after you save it.

## Settings

::shot settings-booking | Booking rules.
1. **Booking rules** and **Prescription letterhead** are the two tabs.
2. **Require a down payment** — a deposit reserves the slot. When it is on, you also set the deposit and the time the patient has to pay.
3. **Free cancellation window** — cancel earlier than this and the patient keeps the deposit.
4. **Appointment length** — one length for every appointment type.
5. **Time zone** — your clinic hours are read in this zone. Contact MedTech to change it.
6. **Save**.

Changes apply to new bookings only. Patients who already booked keep the policy they agreed to.

::shot settings-letterhead | The prescription letterhead.
1. **From your profile and clinic** — your name, specialty, degrees, clinic name, address, phone and logo are filled in for you. Each prescription uses the clinic of its appointment.
2. **Edit profile** / **Edit clinics** — change those details at the source.
3. **Accent colour** — the colour of the letterhead.
4. **Show on the prescription** — switch each item on or off.
5. The preview shows the result.

To sign your prescriptions, switch on **Signature** under **Show on the prescription**, then upload an image of your signature or press **Draw signature**. Press **Save letterhead**. Changes apply to prescriptions saved from now on.

## Patient Support Programme: your places

A support programme gives you a number of patient *places*. Enrolling one patient spends one place.

::shot balance | Coupon balance.
1. One card per programme, with the medicine under its name.
2. **Available** — places you can still spend.
3. **Pending** — patients enrolled who have not had their first dose yet.
4. **Used** — patients who received their first dose.
5. **Request more places** — ask the programme for more.
6. **How places move** — open it to see the rules.
7. **Enroll a patient** starts an enrolment.

| What happens | Where the place goes |
|---|---|
| You enrol a patient | Available → Pending |
| The case is rejected, or the document-upload window expires | Back to Available |
| The first dose is delivered | Pending → Used |
| The case is cancelled before any subsidy was paid | Back to Available |
| The case is cancelled after a subsidy was paid | Stays Used |

::shot request-places | Asking for more places.
1. **Request more places** — the Program Operator grants or declines it.
2. **How many places?** — between 1 and 100.
3. **Note (optional)** — for example, how many patients are waiting.
4. **Send request**.

## Enrol a walk-in patient

Use this for a patient in front of you who has no MedTech app account yet. Open **Give coupon** (or **Enroll a patient** on the balance page).

### 1. Choose how, and the programme

::shot enrol-start | How the patient enrols, and the programme.
1. **Walk-in patient** — no MedTech account yet.
2. **Patient with the app** — see the next part.
3. Each programme shows how many places you have left, and its details (payment model, offer, doses, how the medicine is handed over).
4. A programme you cannot use right now is greyed out, with the reason.

### 2. Confirm who the patient is

::shot enrol-identity | The patient's mobile, code and ID.
1. **Today's walk-in patients** — pick the patient from today's walk-in list to fill in their phone and name. Optional.
2. **Patient's mobile number**, then **Send code**.
3. The code can be sent again after the countdown.
4. **Confirmation code** — the patient reads you the 6-digit code from their phone.
5. **Patient's full name**.
6. **National ID number** — type the 14 digits on the patient's ID card.

The box *No app profile yet? Open one here* opens a profile for a patient who has not registered in the app. If the patient's record is already complete, it is ignored.

### 3. Fill in the enrolment form

::shot enrol-form | The programme's enrolment form.
1. **Diagnosis** — pick from the programme's list.
2. **Prescribed strength**.
3. Tap one answer for each question. A red star means required.
4. **Confirm the tests you asked for** — tick each one. Each becomes a document the patient uploads in the app.
5. What is still missing. The button stays grey until nothing is.
6. **Enroll and send onboarding SMS**.

### 4. Done

::shot enrol-done | The new case.
1. The patient's case opens.
2. The case reference.
3. *Awaiting documents* — the patient now uploads their documents in the app.
4. The deadline for the upload.
5. The confirmation, with your available places before and after.

> [!warn] If the screen says *The patient's registration is incomplete*, the patient's identity record is missing details. Fill in the patient's full name and national ID number in the box above and press the button again, or ask the patient to finish registration in the app. No place is spent until the enrolment goes through.

::shot enrol-from-appointment | Opened from a walk-in appointment.
1. The programme is already chosen when the booking was linked to one.
2. The walk-in patient is already picked…
3. …with their mobile number…
4. …so you press **Send code** and enter the patient's code.
5. Their name is filled in.

## Your cases

::shot cases-list | My PSP cases.
1. **Follow-ups due** — cases waiting for your follow-up form.
2. *Due* — submit it before the date shown. *Submitted* means it is waiting for review.
3. **Search** by patient, programme or coupon.
4. **Status** — show one stage only.
5. The stage of each case. Click a row to open the case.

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| *Awaiting documents* | The patient still has to upload their documents in the app. |
| *Documents submitted* | The programme team will review them. |
| *Under validation* | The programme team is reviewing the case. |
| *Correction requested* | The patient must fix something in the app. |
| *Approved* | The coupon is active. The patient collects each dose. |
| *Rejected* | Final. To try again, enrol the patient again — that starts a new case. |
| *Closed — window expired* | The patient did not upload in time. The place came back to you. |

::shot case-detail | One case.
1. The patient, the case reference, the programme and the medicine.
2. **Enrollment facts** — diagnosis, strength, date and how the patient was enrolled.
3. **Coupon** — its state and the last day it is valid.
4. **Doses** — each dose and where it stands, for example *Received*, *Ready for collection* or *Not issued yet*. *Free dose* marks a dose the patient does not pay for.
5. The follow-up and its result.

### Submit a follow-up

Open a *Due* follow-up from **Follow-ups due**, then press **Submit follow-up**.

::shot follow-up | A follow-up form.
1. **Follow-up 1** — the number of the follow-up.
2. Answer the questions, as in the enrolment form, and tick the tests.
3. **Propose a strength change (optional)** — only if you change what is prescribed. The coordinator applies it once the follow-up is validated.
4. **Submit**. The coordinator reviews it before the next dose opens.

## Assistants

An assistant helps you run one of your clinics. Only you, the doctor, can add, change or remove an assistant.

::shot assistant-add | Adding an assistant.
1. **Full name**.
2. **Phone number** — the assistant signs in with it.
3. **Password** — at least 9 characters, with a number. Give it to your assistant yourself; no e-mail is sent.
4. **Clinic** — an assistant works at one clinic only.
5. **Permissions** — click to open the list of switches.

::shot assistant-permissions | The permissions, grouped.
1. Each group is one kind of work: **Front desk**, **Changes and cancellations**, **Clinic setup**, **Sensitive information**.
2. Payments are always view-only for an assistant.
3. **Support programs (PSP)** — enrolling spends your places.
4. **Enroll patients into support programs for you** starts off. Switch it on only if you want the assistant to enrol.
5. **Create account**.

A new assistant starts with every booking permission on, and programme enrolment off. After you create the account, the assistant appears in the list with the clinic they work at. Open **Permissions** on their row to change the switches, then press **Save**. The bin removes the assistant; they lose access at once.

### What an assistant can and cannot do

| Task | Assistant |
|---|---|
| Approve or reject booking requests | Only with **Approve or reject booking requests** |
| Check patients in | Only with **Check patients in** |
| Start and complete a visit | Only with **Mark a visit complete** |
| Cancel, mark a no-show, emergency cancel | Only with the matching switch under **Changes and cancellations** |
| Run the walk-in list | Only with **Register walk-in patients** |
| Change the booking rules | Only with **Change clinic settings** — otherwise view only |
| See intake answers and medical records | Only with **See patient medical records** |
| See payments | Only with **See payment status**, and never refund |
| Enrol a patient on a support programme | Only with **Enroll patients into support programs for you** |
| Write or edit a prescription | Never — doctors only |
| Change the prescription letterhead | Never |
| Manage assistants | Never |

The other switches (the calendar, the intake questions, reschedule requests, undoing a check-in, the clinic dashboard) work the same way: the assistant can do only what you switch on.

This is what an assistant sees without a permission:

::shot assistant-booking | An appointment, seen by an assistant.
1. The top bar says *Assistant*.
2. Only the buttons the assistant is allowed. Here: **Check in**.
3. **Medical details hidden** — this assistant may not see medical records. Payments are not shown either.

::shot assistant-enrol | Enrolment, seen by an assistant without permission.
1. The assistant is told that the doctor has not switched this permission on.

## Quick answers

**I closed the page in the middle of a form.** Nothing was saved. Start again.

**A patient cannot be enrolled — no places left.** Use **Request more places** on **Coupon balance**.

**The patient's code does not arrive.** Check the mobile number, wait for the countdown, then press **Send again**.

**My new clinic does not appear for patients.** It must be *Approved* by MedTech, and it needs opening hours.

**My assistant cannot see a button.** Open **Assistants**, open their **Permissions**, switch on the task and press **Save**.

**Where do I print a prescription?** Save it, then press **Open PDF** and print from there.
