## Getting started

Download the MedTech app from Google Play or the App Store. The first time you open it, the app asks you to choose your language, English or العربية. You can change it at any time in **Settings**.

A few welcome slides follow; tap Skip to go straight on. If the app asks to update, update it from the store — an old version cannot be used.

## Sign in or create your account

You do not need a password. Your mobile number is your account, and a code sent by SMS signs you in.

::shot app-phone | Log in or sign up with your mobile number.
1. Type your Egyptian mobile number (it starts with 010, 011, 012 or 015).
2. **Continue** — the app sends a 6-digit code to that number by SMS.
3. **Trouble signing in?** helps if the code does not arrive, and shows how to reach support.
4. **العربية** switches the app to Arabic. You can switch back at any time.

By continuing you accept the app's **Terms** and **Privacy Policy**; tap either one to read it.

::shot app-code | Check your messages.
1. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS.
2. **Didn't get it? Resend now.** sends you a new code. Just after a code is sent, it counts down a few seconds first.
3. **Verify** signs you in.
4. **Change number** if you typed the wrong number.

The first time, the app asks a few questions about you:

::shot app-profile | Tell us about yourself.
1. **Add photo** — a profile photo, if you like (JPG or PNG).
2. **Full Name** — required.
3. **Email** — optional.
4. **Birth Date** and **Gender** — both required.
5. **Chronic diseases** — optional; select any that apply.
6. **Create Account** finishes and opens Home.

> [!note] Your national ID is not asked here. It is only needed when you register for a support programme.

## Home

Home always tells you the one thing to do next.

::shot app-home | Home.
1. **Notifications** — the red number shows how many you have not read.
2. **What to do now** — your next step, with a button that takes you there (here **Upload documents**).
3. Anything still waiting for you is marked **Required**.
4. Shortcuts: **My medicine**, **Documents**, **Your doctor appointment**, **My Bookings** and **Find another program**.
5. The tabs at the bottom: **Home**, **My programs** and **My account**.

Pull the screen down to refresh it.

## Support programmes

A support programme gives you your medicine at a reduced price, or free. **Your doctor enrols you** during a visit — you cannot join a programme on your own. After that, you finish the steps in the app.

### Find a programme and its doctors

::shot app-find-program | Find a support program.
1. Type a medicine or an illness under **Medicine or illness** to search.
2. Each programme shows the illness it is for, the **Medicine** it covers and its **Support offer**.
3. **View program** opens the programme: what it needs from you and which doctors work with it.

From a programme you can book a visit with one of its doctors.

### Register your programme profile (once)

Your programme profile is done once and is used for every programme. It has two steps: **Scan & check ID**, then **Complete registration**.

The first time, the app opens the camera. Hold the front of your national ID card inside the frame, then the back; the app checks the light, glare and focus for you. It then reads the card and fills in your details, which you can always correct. The screen below is how it looks once your profile is complete.

::shot app-register-id | Scan and check your ID.
1. This note tells you your profile is complete. You can still update it here.
2. **Both ID photos added · View or retake** — open it to look at the photos of your card, or take them again.
3. **National ID** — your national ID number. Once a programme case uses it, it can no longer be changed.
4. **FROM YOUR NATIONAL ID** — your date of birth, gender, age and place of birth, taken from your card.
5. **Full name (Arabic)** — write it exactly as it is printed on your card — and **Full name in English**.
6. **National ID expiry** — the date your card expires.

> [!warn] An expired ID card cannot be used. Renew the card first.

::shot app-register-form | Complete registration.
1. **Governorate** — where you live. It is used to route you to nearby pharmacies and nurse visits.
2. **Region / area** — a finer location. Pharmacy and home-nurse times are offered for this area. A home-visit address is asked for with each visit and is never saved here.
3. **Use my location** — optional; it helps the programme team choose the pharmacy closest to you.
4. **Confirm your information** — read how your ID number and the coupons you have used are kept, under the fair-use policy.
5. Tick **I agree that the programme sponsor and MedTech may process my data for support programmes.**
6. **Please check: Consent** — what is still missing before you can save. **Show me** takes you to it.

Further down the page you may be asked for your weight and height, and any questions the programme adds. When everything is filled in, save your profile.

### Accept the programme consent

::shot app-consent | Program consent.
1. The programme your doctor enrolled you in, with its medicine.
2. The programme terms — read them to the end.
3. Tick **I have read and agree to this program consent**.
4. **Agree and continue** — it works once the box is ticked.

### Send your documents

::shot app-documents | Program documents.
1. **Upload window closes** — the last day to send your documents.
2. **Needed from you** — a card for each document. **Doctor requested this specifically** marks the ones your doctor asked for.
3. **Upload** — take a photo or choose a file (photo or PDF, up to 20 MB).
4. **Request one deadline extension** — if you need more time. You can ask once.
5. **Submit documents for validation** — uploading a file does not send it. Your documents only count once you submit them.

If a document is rejected, the reason is shown on its card — upload it again and submit.

> [!warn] Send your documents before the date shown. If the time runs out, the case is closed.

### Follow your case

::shot app-my-programs | **My PSP**, from the **My programs** tab.
1. Your programme, its medicine, and where your case is now — here **Approved**.
2. How you receive your medicine — here **At the pharmacy counter**.
3. Your journey: **Registered**, **Joined the program**, **Send your documents**, **Approval**. A tick marks each step that is done.
4. The step that needs you now — here **Collect your medicine**, with the **Collect my medicine** button.
5. **Doses & gift box** and **Documents** open your doses and your documents.

Your finished programmes, and how much you saved in each, are in Program history.

| Your case shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| Awaiting documents | Send the documents the programme asked for. |
| Documents submitted / Under review | The programme team is checking your documents. |
| Information requested | The team needs something more — open **Program documents**. |
| Resubmitted | You sent the correction; the team is checking it. |
| Approved | You can collect your medicine. |
| Rejected | The programme did not accept the case. Your doctor can enrol you again. |
| Closed — window expired | The documents were not sent in time. |

## Collect your medicine

When a dose is ready you get a notification, and Home shows it.

::shot app-medicine | Doses & gift box.
1. **YOUR ENTITLEMENT** — your coupon for the whole course: the **Coupon** number, the **Support** you get, and the date it is **Valid until**.
2. How far you are: doses received out of the doses your course covers (here **Dose 0 of 2**).
3. **Your doses** — every dose of your course, including future ones. Each shows its **Delivery Code**, the date to collect it by, and what you pay.
4. **Where this dose is** — how far the current dose has got, from **Not yet issued** to **Received**.
5. **Collect my medicine** opens the collection screen for this dose.
6. A future dose has no code until it is issued. **FREE** means you pay nothing for that dose.

One coupon covers your whole course, not one dose. Each dose gets its own delivery code, issued one at a time.

### If you are asked to choose a pharmacy

Sometimes the programme team finds more than one pharmacy that has your medicine and lets you choose. The choice appears in **My programs**, marked **Required**.

::shot app-choose-pharmacy | Choose your pharmacy.
1. **Choose your pharmacy** — these pharmacies have your medicine.
2. Tap the pharmacy you will collect from.
3. *Your choice is final. Only the programme team can change it later.*
4. **Choose this pharmacy** confirms it. From then on, you collect as described below.

### At the pharmacy

::shot app-collect | Medicine collection.
1. **Collect from** — the pharmacy to go to, with **Directions** and **Call**.
2. **Amount due at pharmacy** — what you pay at the counter for this dose.
3. What to do for this dose — here **This dose is free**, so you pay nothing.
4. **Show my collection code** — the app sends you an SMS code first; your collection code (QR) then stays on screen for 10 minutes. Show it to the pharmacist with your national ID card.
5. **I have received the drug** — press it only when the medicine is in front of you; the pharmacist then hands it over.
6. **Dose & collection details** — more about this dose.

From your second dose, bring back the box of the previous dose.

The screen follows the pharmacist live. Until the pharmacist has prepared your dose and sent it to you, the confirm button does not work; you get a notification when it does. For a paid dose, the screen asks you to pay the pharmacist first, then confirm.

> [!warn] Confirm only at the counter, with the box in front of you. If the dose is paid in the app, you pay on the payment page that opens; that completes your confirmation.

::shot app-collect-done | Collection confirmed.
1. **Collection confirmed** — the pharmacist can now hand you the medicine.
2. The **Pharmacy**, what you paid (**Patient amount**), how much you saved (**You saved**) and when (**Collected on**).
3. **View collection record** — the full record of what was handed over.

### With a home nurse

Some programmes deliver the dose at home.

::shot app-home-visit | Home visit.
1. Your area, and **Before the visit** — how to prepare, from the programme.
2. *You confirm on your phone right before the nurse gives the dose. Without your confirmation the nurse cannot give it.*
3. **Choose a visit time** — times for the region on your profile only. When none are open, the app says so: look again later.
4. **Where should the nurse come?** — the address, and notes for the nurse if you want. It is used for this visit only and is not saved to your profile.
5. **Book the visit**.

On the day, when the nurse arrives: **The nurse is here — confirm**.

You can change the time of a booked visit. If the time you chose was just taken, the app asks you to choose another.

## Book a doctor visit

Open **My Bookings** from Home, then Find a doctor.

::shot app-find-doctor | Find Doctor.
1. **Search Doctors** — search by doctor name.
2. **Doctors** or **Clinics** — look for a doctor or for a clinic.
3. **Filters & sort** — filter by specialty, governorate and city, and sort by rating, price, earliest time or distance.
4. Each doctor shows the specialty, the number of locations, the **Fees**, the **Next available** time and the rating.

::shot app-doctor-profile | Doctor Profile.
1. The doctor's rating and number of **Reviews**, and the lowest fee.
2. **About** — about the doctor.
3. **Available Clinics** — each clinic with its address, its fee and the type of visit, for example **In Clinic**.
4. **Patient reviews** — what other patients wrote, without their names. **See all** shows every review.
5. **Book Now**.

### Make the booking

1. Choose the clinic and the type of visit.
2. Choose a free time in the next 14 days.
3. Answer the doctor's questions, if they have any.
4. Choose whether the doctor may see your medical history.
5. Read the **Booking policy** and tick **I acknowledge the policy**.
6. Check the **Booking summary** and tap **Send booking request**.

::shot app-booking-policy | Booking policy.
1. **Cancellation window** — cancel more than this before your appointment and your deposit comes back in full. Later than that, the deposit is lost.
2. **Deposit** — paid only after the doctor approves your request, never now. The rest of the fee is paid at the clinic.
3. **Slot release** — once the doctor approves, the time is held for you this long. If the deposit is not paid by then, the time goes back to the clinic.
4. Tick **I acknowledge the policy**.
5. **Continue**.

::shot app-booking-summary | Booking summary.
1. Check the doctor, the **Clinic**, the **Date** and **Time**, and the **Appointment Type**.
2. **Consultation fee**, **Deposit due** and **Remaining balance** (paid at the clinic).
3. If the doctor approves, you are asked to pay the deposit to confirm the booking.
4. **Send booking request** — sends the request to the doctor. No money is taken now.

> [!note] A booking is a request until the doctor approves it. If a deposit is needed, you pay it after the approval, before the deadline — otherwise the booking is cancelled.

### Your bookings

::shot app-my-bookings | My Bookings.
1. **Upcoming** and **Past** bookings.
2. Each booking shows its status — here **Completed**. Others may be waiting for the doctor's approval, waiting for your deposit, or confirmed.
3. Tap a booking to see its details and what you can do.

::shot app-booking-details | Your appointment.
1. Where the booking is now — here **Completed**.
2. The doctor, the clinic, the **Date**, **Time**, **Appointment Type** and **Consultation fee**.
3. **Rate your visit** — appears once the visit is complete.
4. **Booking progress** — each step of this booking so far.
5. **About your visit & booking choices** — your answers to the doctor's questions and the choices you made when booking.

Before the visit, this screen also shows what you can do next: pay the deposit once the doctor approves, reschedule (a new time needs the doctor's approval again), or cancel. After a cancellation you can get your deposit back, or move it to a booking with another doctor. Before you cancel, the app tells you whether your deposit will come back.

### After the visit

::shot app-prescription | Your prescription.
1. The medicines with dose, frequency, duration and instructions.
2. Open the prescription document.

::shot app-review | How was your visit?
1. Tap a number from 1 to 5 to rate the visit.
2. **Comment** — **Optional**, up to 500 characters. Other patients can read it.
3. **Submit review**. You can change or delete it for 24 hours.

## Notifications

::shot app-notifications | Notifications.
1. How many you have not read, and **Mark all read**.
2. **All**, **Unread** and **PSP** — every notification, only the unread ones, or only those about your programme.
3. Tap a notification to open what it is about. A blue dot marks the ones you have not read.
4. **Mark as read** — for one notification.

Swipe a notification to delete it. Choose which notifications you get in **Settings** → **Notification preferences**: appointments, medicines, programmes and offers.

## My account

::shot app-account | My account.
1. **Personal details** — your details and your programme profile.
2. **My addresses** — home, work and other saved places.
3. **Medical devices** — connect a supported Bluetooth device (blood pressure, temperature, oxygen, weight) and save its readings.
4. **My reviews** — the ratings and comments you left. **Help Center** is just below.
5. **Settings** — language, theme and security.
6. **Logout**.

::shot app-settings | Settings.
1. **Change phone number** — move your account to a new number. It needs a new SMS code.
2. **Language** — **English** or **Arabic**.
3. **Appearance** — **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your phone.
4. **Accessibility** — display, vision and motion options, such as bigger or bold text and high contrast.
5. **Currency** — prices are shown in Egyptian pounds (EGP).
6. **Notification preferences** — choose which notifications you get.

Further down are app lock, which uses your face or fingerprint so only you can open the app, and deleting your account, which also needs a new SMS code.

## Quick answers

**I changed my phone.** Install the app and sign in with the same mobile number. If your number changed, use **Change phone number** while you still have the old one, or contact support from **Trouble signing in?**.

**Can I join a programme myself?** No. A doctor who works with the programme enrols you after a visit.

**The pharmacy says my code is not there.** Check you are at the pharmacy shown in the app. If your code expired, contact programme support from the collection screen.

**I lost my connection.** You stay signed in. A banner tells you when you are offline; try again when you are back online.
