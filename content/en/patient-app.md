## Getting started

Download the MedTech app from Google Play or the App Store. The first time you open it:

::shot app-language | Choose your language.
1. Pick **English** or **العربية**. You can change it later in Settings.
2. **Continue**.

A few welcome slides follow; tap **Skip** to go straight on. If the app asks to update, update it from the store — an old version cannot be used.

## Sign in or create your account

You do not need a password. Your mobile number is your account, and a code sent by SMS signs you in.

::shot app-phone | Your mobile number.
1. Type your Egyptian mobile number (it starts with 010, 011, 012 or 015).
2. **Send code**.
3. **Trouble signing in?** helps if the code does not arrive, and shows how to reach support.

::shot app-code | The code from your messages.
1. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS.
2. If no code arrives, you can ask for a new one after 30 seconds.

The first time, the app asks a few questions about you:

::shot app-profile | Tell us about yourself.
1. Your full name.
2. Gender and date of birth.
3. Email and long-term illnesses — both optional.
4. **Continue** to Home.

> [!note] Your national ID is not asked here. It is only needed when you register for a support programme.

## Home

Home always tells you the one thing to do next.

::shot app-home | Home.
1. The bell shows how many notifications you have not read.
2. **What to do now** — your next step. Tap it to go there.
3. Things still waiting for you: consent, missing documents, or medicine ready to collect.
4. Shortcuts: **My medicine**, **Documents**, **Program doctors**, **Your appointment**, **My Bookings**.
5. The tabs at the bottom: **Home**, **My programs**, **My account**.

Pull the screen down to refresh it.

## Support programmes

A support programme gives you your medicine at a reduced price, or free. **Your doctor enrols you** during a visit — you cannot join a programme on your own. After that, you finish the steps in the app.

### Find a programme and its doctors

::shot app-find-program | Find a support program.
1. Search by the programme, your medicine, or your illness.
2. Open a programme to see what it needs from you and which doctors work with it.
3. From a programme you can book a visit with one of its doctors.

### Register your programme profile (once)

Your programme profile is done once and is used for every programme. It has two steps: **Scan & check ID**, then **Complete registration**.

::shot app-register-id | Scan your national ID card.
1. Hold the front of the card inside the frame. The app checks the light, glare and focus for you.
2. Then the back of the card.
3. The app reads the card and fills in your details. You can always correct them.

::shot app-register-form | Complete registration.
1. Check your national ID number and your full Arabic name (four names).
2. Weight, height, governorate and city.
3. Any questions the programme adds.
4. Confirm the details are correct and accept the consent, then save.

> [!warn] An expired ID card cannot be used. Renew the card first.

Sharing your location is optional; it helps the programme choose a pharmacy near you.

### Accept the programme consent

::shot app-consent | Consent & terms.
1. Read the programme terms.
2. Tick **I have read and accept the program terms.**

### Send your documents

::shot app-documents | Program documents.
1. One place for each document the programme or your doctor asks for.
2. Take a photo or choose a file for each one (photo or PDF, up to 20 MB).
3. If a document was rejected, the reason is shown here — send it again.
4. **Submit documents for validation** — your documents only count once you submit them.
5. Need more time? **Request one deadline extension** — you can ask once.

> [!warn] Send your documents before the date shown. If the time runs out, the case is closed.

### Follow your case

::shot app-my-programs | My programs.
1. Your programme case and where it is now.
2. The coupon: the whole treatment course.
3. The current dose.
4. Your journey: **Registered**, **Joined the program**, **Send your documents**, **Approval**, **Collect your medicine**.
5. **Program history** shows your finished programmes and how much you saved.

| Your case shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| Awaiting documents | Send the documents the programme asked for. |
| Documents submitted / Under review | The programme team is checking your documents. |
| Information requested | The team needs something more — open **Program documents**. |
| Resubmitted | You sent the correction; the team is checking it. |
| Approved | You can collect your medicine. |
| Rejected | The programme did not accept the case. Your doctor can enrol you again. |
| Closed — window expired | The documents were not sent in time. |

## Collect your medicine

When a dose is ready you get a notification, and Home shows it.

::shot app-medicine | My medicine.
1. How far you are in your course: doses received out of doses covered.
2. Every dose of your course, including future ones.
3. Each dose shows whether it is free or what you pay.

### If you are asked to choose a pharmacy

Sometimes the programme finds more than one pharmacy that has your medicine and lets you choose. You get a notification, and the choice shows on your programme.

::shot app-choose-pharmacy | Choose your pharmacy.
1. The pharmacies that have your medicine.
2. Choose the one that suits you. Your choice is final; if you need a different pharmacy later, contact programme support.

### At the pharmacy

::shot app-collect | Medicine collection.
1. The pharmacy to go to, with **Directions** and **Call**.
2. **Show my collection code** — the app sends you an SMS code first; the collection code then stays visible for 10 minutes.
3. Show the code (QR) to the pharmacist, with your national ID card. From your second dose, bring back the box of the previous dose.
4. The amount due, or that this dose is free.

The screen follows the pharmacist live. When the pharmacist has prepared the dose, a button appears for you to confirm.

> [!warn] Confirm only at the counter, with the box in front of you. If the dose is paid in the app, you pay on the payment page that opens; that completes your confirmation.

::shot app-collect-done | Collection confirmed.
1. **Collection confirmed** — the pharmacist can now hand you the medicine.
2. **View collection record** shows what was handed over and how much you saved.

### With a home nurse

Some programmes deliver the dose at home.

::shot app-home-visit | Home visit.
1. **Choose a visit time** from the times offered for your area.
2. Enter the address for this visit.
3. **Book the visit**.
4. On the day, when the nurse arrives: **The nurse is here — confirm**.

You can change the time of a booked visit. If the time you chose was just taken, the app asks you to choose another.

## Book a doctor visit

Open **My Bookings** from Home, then **Find a doctor**.

::shot app-find-doctor | Find a doctor.
1. Search by doctor name or specialty — or switch to **Clinics**.
2. Filter by specialty, governorate and city.
3. Sort by rating, price, earliest time or distance.
4. Each doctor shows the rating, the fee and the next free time.

::shot app-doctor-profile | The doctor's page.
1. About the doctor, their clinics and fees.
2. Reviews from other patients (anonymous).
3. **Book now**.

### Make the booking

1. Choose the clinic and the **Visit Type**.
2. Choose a free time in the next 14 days.
3. Answer the doctor's questions, if they have any.
4. Choose whether the doctor may see your medical history.
5. Read the **Booking policy** and tick **I acknowledge the policy**.
6. Check the **Booking summary** and send it.

::shot app-booking-policy | Booking policy.
1. **Deposit** — how much you pay in advance, if anything.
2. **Cancellation window** — cancel before this to get the deposit back.
3. How long you have to pay after the doctor approves.
4. **I acknowledge the policy**.

::shot app-booking-summary | Booking summary.
1. **Date & time**.
2. **Consultation fee**, **Deposit due** and **Remaining balance** (paid at the clinic).
3. **Confirm booking** — sends the request. No money is taken now.

> [!note] A booking is a request until the doctor approves it. If a deposit is needed, you pay it after the approval, before the deadline — otherwise the booking is cancelled.

### Your bookings

::shot app-my-bookings | My Bookings.
1. **Upcoming** and **Past** bookings.
2. Each booking shows its status, for example waiting for the doctor's approval, waiting for the deposit, or confirmed.
3. Open a booking to see its details and what you can do.

::shot app-booking-details | A booking.
1. Where the booking is now.
2. **Pay deposit** — once the doctor approves.
3. **Reschedule** or cancel. A new time needs the doctor's approval again.
4. After a cancellation: **Refund deposit**, or **Book another doctor** to move the deposit to them.

Before you cancel, the app tells you whether your deposit will come back.

### After the visit

::shot app-prescription | Your prescription.
1. The medicines with dose, frequency, duration and instructions.
2. Open the prescription document.

::shot app-review | Rate your visit.
1. Give 1 to 5 stars.
2. Add a comment if you like (up to 500 characters).
3. **Submit review**. You can change or delete it for 24 hours.

## Notifications

::shot app-notifications | Notifications.
1. **All**, **Unread** and programme notifications.
2. **Mark all read**.
3. Tap a notification to open what it is about. Swipe to delete it.

Choose which notifications you get in **Settings → Notifications**: appointments, payments, programmes and offers.

## My account

::shot app-account | My account.
1. **Personal details** — your details and your programme profile.
2. **My addresses** — add, edit or choose your default address.
3. **Devices** — take a reading from a supported Bluetooth device (blood pressure, temperature, oxygen, weight) and save it.
4. **My reviews**, **Help center** and **Settings**.
5. **Logout**.

::shot app-settings | Settings.
1. **Language** — English or العربية.
2. **Appearance** — light, dark, or like your phone.
3. **Accessibility** — bigger or bold text, high contrast and more.
4. App lock with your face or fingerprint, so only you can open the app.
5. **Change phone number** and **Delete Account** — both need a new SMS code.

## Quick answers

**I changed my phone.** Install the app and sign in with the same mobile number. If your number changed, use **Change phone number** while you still have the old one, or contact support from **Trouble signing in?**.

**Can I join a programme myself?** No. A doctor who works with the programme enrols you after a visit.

**The pharmacy says my code is not there.** Check you are at the pharmacy shown in the app. If your code expired, contact programme support from the collection screen.

**I lost my connection.** You stay signed in. A banner tells you when you are offline; try again when you are back online.
