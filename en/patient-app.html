MedTech patient app

Join your support programme, send its documents, collect each dose, and book doctor visits — all from your phone.

For patients using the MedTech app on Android or iPhone.

Getting started

Download the MedTech app from Google Play or the App Store. The first time you open it:

A few welcome slides follow; tap Skip to go straight on. If the app asks to update, update it from the store — an old version cannot be used.

Sign in or create your account

You do not need a password. Your mobile number is your account, and a code sent by SMS signs you in.

The first time, the app asks a few questions about you:

Note Your national ID is not asked here. It is only needed when you register for a support programme.

Home

Home always tells you the one thing to do next.

Pull the screen down to refresh it.

Support programmes

A support programme gives you your medicine at a reduced price, or free. Your doctor enrols you during a visit — you cannot join a programme on your own. After that, you finish the steps in the app.

Find a programme and its doctors

Register your programme profile (once)

Your programme profile is done once and is used for every programme. It has two steps: Scan & check ID, then Complete registration.

Important An expired ID card cannot be used. Renew the card first.

Sharing your location is optional; it helps the programme choose a pharmacy near you.

Accept the programme consent

Send your documents

Important Send your documents before the date shown. If the time runs out, the case is closed.

Collect your medicine

When a dose is ready you get a notification, and Home shows it.

If you are asked to choose a pharmacy

Sometimes the programme finds more than one pharmacy that has your medicine and lets you choose. You get a notification, and the choice shows on your programme.

At the pharmacy

The screen follows the pharmacist live. When the pharmacist has prepared the dose, a button appears for you to confirm.

Important Confirm only at the counter, with the box in front of you. If the dose is paid in the app, you pay on the payment page that opens; that completes your confirmation.

With a home nurse

Some programmes deliver the dose at home.

You can change the time of a booked visit. If the time you chose was just taken, the app asks you to choose another.

Book a doctor visit

Open My Bookings from Home, then Find a doctor.

Make the booking

  1. Choose the clinic and the Visit Type.
  2. Choose a free time in the next 14 days.
  3. Answer the doctor's questions, if they have any.
  4. Choose whether the doctor may see your medical history.
  5. Read the Booking policy and tick I acknowledge the policy.
  6. Check the Booking summary and send it.
Note A booking is a request until the doctor approves it. If a deposit is needed, you pay it after the approval, before the deadline — otherwise the booking is cancelled.

Your bookings

Before you cancel, the app tells you whether your deposit will come back.

Quick answers

I changed my phone. Install the app and sign in with the same mobile number. If your number changed, use Change phone number while you still have the old one, or contact support from Trouble signing in?.

Can I join a programme myself? No. A doctor who works with the programme enrols you after a visit.

The pharmacy says my code is not there. Check you are at the pharmacy shown in the app. If your code expired, contact programme support from the collection screen.

I lost my connection. You stay signed in. A banner tells you when you are offline; try again when you are back online.
